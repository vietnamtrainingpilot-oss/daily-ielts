## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for uncompromising clarity, intellectual gravity, and focused discipline. Built for test candidates seeking high band scores, it rejects casual gamification, juvenile celebrations, floating confetti, and patronizing streak graphics. Instead, it channels the austere functional excellence of official Cambridge assessment papers, British Council examination terminals, and high-precision productivity software like Linear and Things 3.

### Design Principles
- **Scholarly Authority:** Surfaces mimic premium heavy-stock examination paper and clinical digital testing environments. Visual weight comes from precise typography and ruled partitions rather than decorative styling.
- **Distraction-Free Gatekeeping:** UI chrome recedes completely during focus sprints and passage reading. Elements are structured to lower cognitive fatigue and elevate mental endurance.
- **Institutional Trust:** Information is organized with institutional precision: clear question indices, tabular band-score notation (e.g., Band 8.5), and unambiguous system locks.
- **Swiss Precision Layout:** Strict adherence to grid alignment, restrained contrast ratios, and structural hairline rules that anchor focus without shouting.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary authority of **Newsreader** for editorial headings and continuous long-form passage reading with the neutral engineering precision of **Inter** for questions, labels, interface controls, and numerical outputs.

Both families are **bundled as local assets**, never fetched at runtime. An app whose purpose is to gate a device offline must not depend on a network font download to render its own lock screen.

### Editorial & Reading Content
- **Newsreader** simulates the typographic dignity of academic publishing. Use `body-lg` with loose line-height (`30px`) for simulated IELTS Academic Reading passages to prevent visual crowding and support comfortable scanning.
- Section prompts, passage titles, and band overview headings leverage `headline-xl` through `headline-md` in regular-to-semibold weights.

### Systems, Metadata & Numbers
- **Inter** handles all micro-copy, navigation, form controls, and task instructions.
- All scores, countdown clocks, test question counters (e.g., `Question 14 of 40`), and percentile tables must enforce OpenType tabular figures (`fontFeatures: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during countdowns and data updates.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured, balanced grid system optimized for side-by-side reading and split-pane execution during rigorous study modes.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed 12-column grid container capped at 1280px max-width, using `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. When engaged in mock exam mode, the layout switches to a synchronized 50/50 dual-pane architecture: left pane fixed for source reading/transcripts, right pane scrollable for answer inputs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with split panes toggling between tabbed master-detail views or vertical split with persistent sticky question navigation.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Persistent top-anchored timer banner and bottom-anchored question progress bar.

### Spacing Discipline
- Maintain strict alignment along a baseline multiple of 4px.
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metadata clusters (e.g., band badge icons, time chips).
- Use `space-md` for internal component padding and input-group gap spacing.
- Use `space-lg` and `space-xl` for delineating major testing sections and habit verification panes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural hairline borders, crisp surface contrasts, and tonal tiering rather than heavy drop shadows. The design evokes physical sheets of bound paper laid systematically across a desk.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Global Backdrop):** `#F8F9FA` (Off-white paper substrate).
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an unequivocal `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. No drop shadow.
- **Surface Level 2 (Active Focus / Active Question Block):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with `1.5px solid #1E3A8A`. A low-opacity, structural ambient shadow softens the edge: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals, Locked Habit Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` framed by `#0F172A`, floating above a dense backdrop tint `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.60)` to enforce complete focus gating.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a restrained geometric discipline with soft, minimal rounding (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Controls & Form Elements:** Standard buttons, text inputs, segmented tabs, and test cards feature a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This conveys architectural solidity and seriousness.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** Band markers and skill tags maintain the same `0.25rem` radius or strict rectangular profile. Pill-shaped shapes are strictly forbidden to avoid looking like social media tags or games.
- **Passage & Sheet Cards:** Outer reading sheets and mock test containers employ a clean `0.5rem` (8px) maximum radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Exam / Session Proceed):** Solid Deep Navy (`#1E3A8A`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` corner radius, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#0F172A`. Zero elevation.
- **Secondary (Inspect / Mark for Review):** Transparent surface with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border, `#0F172A` text. Hover brings `#F1F5F9` background.
- **Locked / Gating Action:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#94A3B8`, cursor `not-allowed`, accompanied by a lock icon.

### Cards & Reading Sheets
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, bounded by a uniform `1px solid #E2E8F0` edge. No blur or rounded drops.
- Reading comprehension containers feature generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) with line lengths strictly clamped to 65–75 characters for optimal legibility.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Input fields feature a `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #CBD5E1` boundary, and 4px border radius.
- On focus: crisp border color switch to `#1E3A8A` with a clean `2px` ring in `rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.12)`. No vibrant glows.
- Inline IELTS sentence completion fields are styled as understated bottom-line inputs (`border-bottom: 1.5px solid #64748B`) set in tabular-proportional font.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Multiple Choice
- **Multiple Choice Options:** Full-width block selectors with light border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Selection state triggers a solid `#1E3A8A` border with a subtle background tint (`rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.04)`).
- Radio markers use crisp circular targets with an internal navy dot; checkboxes use precise square indicators with a geometric check icon.

### Badges, Band Scores & Chips
- **Band Score Badges:** Clean rectangular boxes with tabular numerics (e.g., `8.5`). Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600.
- **Skill Tags:** Muted background tints with high-contrast text, per the skill identifiers above.

### Habit Gate / Exam Progress Indicators
- **Countdown Clock:** Fixed or persistent floating block displaying tabular elapsed time in crimson (`#BE185D`) or navy (`#0F172A`).
- **Question Matrix:** A 40-cell grid representing test questions. Unanswered questions remain neutral outlined squares (`#E2E8F0`); answered items fill solid `#1E293B`; items marked for review show an amber accent dot (`#B45309`).
